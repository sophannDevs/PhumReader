# PhumiReader – story checklist

Your stories, from the first idea to a published episode. Work through it on story days (Thursday: writing,
Saturday: production, see `docs/WEEKLY_PLAN.md`).

**Goal before launch:** 10 finished episodes of Series 1, uploaded as drafts or scheduled. Optional: 3 episodes of
Series 2.
**Goal after launch:** keep at least 4 finished episodes ahead of the schedule. If the buffer drops below 4, don't
start anything new until it's back.

---

## 1. How one episode is made

Every episode goes through the same 7 steps. Do them in order and finish one before starting the next.

| Step | What | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Outline | What happens in this episode, in 5–10 lines | You know the start, the turn and the hook at the end |
| 2. Script | Panel by panel: what we see, who says what | Every panel has a description; dialogue in English and Khmer |
| 3. Storyboard | Rough sketches of the panels and the scroll flow | The whole episode reads well when you scroll it quickly |
| 4. Art | Clean drawings and colour | Every panel is finished |
| 5. Lettering | Speech bubbles and sound effects | Text checked in both languages |
| 6. Upload | Creator portal → Upload episode, saved as a draft | Preview looks right on a phone |
| 7. Schedule | Publish date and time (Phnom Penh time), Fast Pass setting | The episode shows "Scheduled" |

### Check before you upload (step 6)
- [ ] Panels are JPG or PNG, at least **800 px wide** (narrower panels are refused).
- [ ] Each image is under 20 MB; at most 100 images and 200 MB for the whole episode.
- [ ] Series cover is at least 600 px wide; banner at least 800 px; thumbnail square, at least 300 px.
- [ ] Spelling checked in English and in Khmer.
- [ ] Episode title and creator's note filled in.
- [ ] Fast Pass: default is 5 coins and free after 7 days (you can set 3–10 coins and 1–90 days).
- [ ] Comments on or off, as you want.
- [ ] Previewed on a phone (390 px wide): no text cut off.

## 5. How Claude can help with stories

You decide the story. Claude can do the slow parts:
- **Outline:** "Here is my story bible and what happened in episode 3. Give me 3 options for episode 4."
- **Script draft:** "Turn this outline into a panel-by-panel script, about 40 panels, with English dialogue."
- **Khmer check:** "Check this Khmer dialogue for spelling and natural speech." (A native reader still checks it.)
- **Consistency:** "Read episodes 1–5 and list anything that contradicts the story bible."

If you use the same requests every week, they can become a skill later.
